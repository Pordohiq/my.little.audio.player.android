# Welcome to my.little.audio.player.android

This is a little audio player with a nice UI. The whole project was a long journey for me but also a dream of mine, that I have had 3 years before the release. I have learned very much about Applications, Frameworks and Programming in general so I am very grateful that I had the patience to finish this project. I now use it as my Audio Player.

## Why an Audio Player

I am a person, who doesn't have money and especially doesn't have money to spend on things like Spotify or YouTube Music. That's why I downloaded all my Audio Files from a online Site with a white play button on red background. I have all my audio files stored locally. I was using Files by Google to play back my audio. I ofc ran into multiple issues of which my main problem was that there was no folder structure in the Audio tab and to get to the folder structure was a long way. That's why my Audio Player works with a Ressource Tree (ResTree) in which all audio files and folders are nocely ordered.

## Where are the icons from?
I begann this audio player creation journey in Godot. I really liked there icon set so I just used their icons inside my application. Not all of the icons fit directly so I had to open up inkscape and mix a few icons occasionally. Personally I am very happy with the icon set. For licensing concerns, please visit: https://godotengine.org/license/

## Why Java?
I started this project in Godot, than migrated to Flutter and finally decided to go fully native on Android with java. My goal is to always have the smallest and most compatible executables possible, so the obvious answer is to just just the native way of doing things. That does mean, that I will have to create another codebase for iOS from scratch, but I don't worry about it that much.
